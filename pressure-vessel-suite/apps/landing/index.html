Pressure design

ASME Section VIII Division 1 coverage includes cylindrical shells, formed heads, flat covers, conical sections, external pressure/vacuum, nozzle reinforcement, minimum thickness, test pressure and MDMT. Global MAWP follows the governing component.

04 / FAQ

Clear answers.

Important details before you start.

Is it free?

Yes. The beta is free and you can open the app without creating an account.

Which codes are covered?

ASME Section VIII Division 1 calculations and EN 13445 shell, head and test pressure calculations. EN 13445 can be selected alongside ASME.

Is PV Suite certified?

No. It is not a certified or stamped tool. Results are preliminary and must be reviewed by a qualified engineer.

Can I import CAD?

STEP import recognises shell, heads and radial nozzles, then suggests values for you to confirm. STL is view-only.

What data do you store?

No account is required, so we do not collect personal account data. In V1, calculations are not stored server-side long-term.

Who builds it?

PV Suite is built by ZeysLabs. Questions? Contact us.
